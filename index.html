04 / The operating doctrine

Deliberate.
By design.

I / OBSERVE

Find the real signal.

Understand the work before changing it. Examine the constraints. Separate useful evidence from comfortable assumptions.

II / CALIBRATE

Earn the next step.

Test a focused response. Measure what changes. Keep the next commitment proportionate to what you know.

III / CONTINUE

Leave something durable.

Define ownership. Document the handoff. Build systems that remain useful beyond the person who created them.
