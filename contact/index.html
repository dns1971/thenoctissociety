A FEW USEFUL DETAILS

Before we begin.

Do I need to know which tools I want?

No. Start with the process and the outcome. Tool selection comes after understanding the work.

Can I bring an early-stage idea?

Yes. Describe the problem, who experiences it, and what you already know. Early discussions should define a useful test before committing to a larger build.

Are all listed initiatives available to customers?

No. Initiative stages are shown on the home page. Development and exploration projects are not presented as launched services.
